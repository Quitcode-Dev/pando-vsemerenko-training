# US-002: Contributor Profile Questionnaire (Tap-First)

Project: Pando
Epic: Unassigned
Status: approved
Priority: must
Effort: M

**As a** founding contributor
**I want** to complete a short tap-first profile questionnaire covering my neighborhood, child ages, and community affiliations
**So that** my recommendations can be matched to parents whose life context is similar to mine

## Acceptance Criteria

- The questionnaire is completable in under 60 seconds on a mobile device
- Neighborhood and child age are required fields; all other fields are optional
- All answer options are presented as tappable selections, not free-text inputs by default
- Neighborhood and school options are pre-populated with Pasadena-specific values
- A contributor cannot advance past the profile step without selecting at least a neighborhood and one child age
- An 'Other' free-text entry is available on all multi-choice fields and is saved as a pending taxonomy option for admin review
- Completed profile data is stored against the contributor record with a profile_completeness flag set to true when both required fields are present
- The questionnaire renders without horizontal scrolling on screens 375px wide and above
