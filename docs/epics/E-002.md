# E-002: Contributor Onboarding & Profile Questionnaire

Project: Pando
Status: approved
Priority: must

Deliver a tap-first, mobile-optimised Social Affinity and Life Relevance profile questionnaire that founding contributors complete in under 60 seconds. This epic captures the structured profile data required for two-layer matching and populates Pasadena-specific options for neighborhoods, schools, groups, and activities.

## Acceptance Criteria

• Questionnaire is completable in under 60 seconds on a mobile device
• Neighborhood and child age are required fields; all other fields are optional
• Pre-populated options reflect Pasadena-specific neighborhoods, schools, groups, and classes
• Profile captures social affinity dimensions (schools, neighborhoods, groups, activities) and life-relevance dimensions (budget posture, logistics, family setup, trust circles)
• Contributors can select 'Other' for any field; these entries are flagged for admin review
• Profile completeness score is stored per contributor record
• Questionnaire experience is conversational and does not feel like a form or survey
• Partial completions are saved so contributors do not lose progress
