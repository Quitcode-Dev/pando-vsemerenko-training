# E-008: Analytics, Monitoring & KPI Reporting

Project: Pando
Status: approved
Priority: should

Provide the Pando team with the observability and reporting needed to track Phase 1 and Phase 2 KPIs, identify drop-off points, and make data-informed decisions about product quality and contributor health throughout the pilot. This epic covers funnel analytics, contributor health metrics, and answer composition reporting.

## Acceptance Criteria

• Phase 1 funnel metrics are trackable: onboarding completion rate, contributors with 2+ recommendations, total approved activities, caregiver nominations, blast opt-in rate, profile completeness rate
• Phase 2 metrics are trackable: repeat query rate per user, % of queries answered with human-truth layer, Blast response rate, paid Blast conversion rate, contributor retention rate, thanks delivery rate, contributor opt-out rate
• Admin dashboard surfaces key KPI indicators without requiring direct database access
• Drop-off points in the Phase 1 seed conversation funnel are identifiable
• Answer composition labels (human-truth vs. public/general) are stored per conversation and reportable
• Contributor health metrics (response rate, tier status, opt-out events) are visible per contributor
• All KPI targets can be updated by the Pando team once confirmed values are set
