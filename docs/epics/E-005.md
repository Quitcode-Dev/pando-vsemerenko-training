# E-005: SMS Query Handling & Answer Retrieval

Project: Pando
Status: approved
Priority: must

Enable querying parents to text Pando a natural-language question and receive a structured, labelled answer drawn from the human-truth graph without downloading an app or creating an account. This epic covers intent classification, clarifying question logic, answer retrieval, trust and freshness labelling, and caregiver answer enforcement.

## Acceptance Criteria

• Parents can text a US phone number to query Pando with no app or account required
• Pando may ask one clarifying question to capture life-relevance context before answering
• Queries are classified by intent (activity, caregiver, general)
• Answers are retrieved from the human-truth graph using two-layer matching (social affinity + life relevance)
• Every answer component is labelled with provenance, freshness, and validation count
• Public or general information is clearly labelled and never presented as human-trust data
• Caregiver answers are only returned when consent_status = consented AND active = true; this is enforced at the query level
• When no qualifying caregiver exists, Pando honestly states this and offers a Last Minute Care Blast
• Pando presents a Blast offer when answers are absent, stale, or weak
• Answer composition labels are stored per conversation for KPI measurement
